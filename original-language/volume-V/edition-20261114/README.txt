This reader contains Latin and German writings on attraction, mechanics, equilibrium of fluids, absolute magnetic intensity, the theory of terrestrial magnetism, potential theory, dioptrics, magnetic instruments and observations, mathematical physics, and material from the mathematical-physics Nachlass.
