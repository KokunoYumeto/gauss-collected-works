Carl Friedrich Gauss, \emph{Werke}, sechster Band (Göttingen, 1874), issued by the Königliche Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen. The historical text preserves the printed attributions, including Ernst Schering's concluding remarks.

The inherited LaTeX transcription and typesetting are credited to OpenAI GPT-6 Astra. Source comparisons and LaTeX corrections are AI-produced, including work by GPT-6.1 Sol.

The reader follows source leaves 2--669 (through printed page 664), including the historical titles, portrait, concluding remarks, contents and printer's imprint. Twelve leaves without historical print appear as blank pages. Material omitted by the collected edition is outside this transcription. The LaTeX reader uses modern type. Some source marks have qualified readings.
