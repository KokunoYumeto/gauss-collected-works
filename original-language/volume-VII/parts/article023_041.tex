Si pro inveniendo tempore ex anomalia vera quantitate auxiliari $u$ uti placuerit, huius valor commodissime per aequ. IV determinatur; formula dein II absque novo calculo statim dat $p$ per $r$, vel $r$ per $p$. Inventa $u$ formula XI dabit quantitatem $\frac{\lambda k t}{b^{\frac{3}{2}}}$, quae analoga est anomaliae mediae in ellipsi et per $N$ denotabitur, unde demanabit tempus post transitum per perihelium elapsum. Quum pars prior ipsius $N$ puta $\frac{\lambda e(u u-1)}{2u}$ per formulam VIII fiat $=\frac{\lambda r\sin v}{b\sin\psi}$, calculus duplex huius quantitatis ipsius praecisioni examinandae inservire, aut si mavis, $N$ absque $u$ ita exhiberi potest
\[
\mathrm{XII.}\quad N=\frac{\lambda\operatorname{tang}\psi\sin v}{2\cos\frac{1}{2}(v+\psi)\cos\frac{1}{2}(v-\psi)}-\log\frac{\cos\frac{1}{2}(v-\psi)}{\cos\frac{1}{2}(v+\psi)}\cdot
\]
