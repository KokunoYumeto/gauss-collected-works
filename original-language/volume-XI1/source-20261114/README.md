Native leaves 2, 3, 6, 8–200, 202, 204–224, 226–262, 264 and 266–275, with leaf 276 through the end of Göttingen diary entry [4], before [5]. This includes the opening titles, both Hannover measurement reports, mechanics correspondence, magnetic report, twelve magnetic Nachlass pieces, thirty magnetic letters, Dioptrik I–VI with the historical explanations, all twenty-four Dioptrik letters and the photometry appendix, the acoustics letter to W. Weber, all three electrical and magnetic letters to W. Weber with the telegraph key and three electrostatic figures, and the complete printed “Zurückführung der Wechselwirkungen zwischen galvanischen Strömen und Magnetismus auf absolute Maasse”: Articles 1–11, Figure 4 and Schaefer’s historical remark. The chronology title and complete publications [I]–[III] follow: the Easter-date method, its correction, and the excerpts from P. Tittel’s De Calendario ecclesiastico, with Loewy’s historical remarks. All three chronology Nachlass pieces follow, on weekdays, the golden number, epacts, Easter dates and the new moon of Tisri, including the Praecepta universalia and Loewy’s historical remarks. The theoretical-astronomy title and the complete Elliptische Bahnbestimmung, Nachlass [I], follow: the notation, first approximation from three observations, calculation of the orbital elements from two geocentric positions, determination of the orbit and time, geocentric formulas, three improvement methods and differential formulas. Further contents are the complete Nachlass [II], Weiteres zur Bahnbestimmung, with its prescriptions and observation examples; Nachlass [III], Zur Bahnbestimmung der Ceres, with all four hypotheses and Brendel’s historical remarks; and the circular-orbit method after W. Klinkerfues, with its geometric figure and equations (1)–(23). The practical-astronomy title, both Braunschweig observation sections with their historical tables, five letters to Olbers and Brendel’s remarks, and Göttingen diary entries [1]–[4] from December 1808 to October 1809 follow. Göttingen entry [5] onward and the general title with its portrait are outside this selection.
