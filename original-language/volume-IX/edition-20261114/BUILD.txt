Building the editable LaTeX source

Use a LaTeX distribution with pdfLaTeX, UTF-8 input and T1 font support,
ngerman Babel, Latin Modern, amsmath, amssymb, longtable, pdflscape, TikZ,
microtype, geometry, graphicx and hyperref. The supplied delimiter helper uses
pdfTeX primitives and is included by main.tex.

Extract the complete source archive. Keep both source directories:

    20261110_native310_311/
    20261110_native311b_onward/

Article [4] is in the first directory; main.tex and the remaining sources
are in the second. The relative include of Article [4] depends on this
sibling-directory layout.

From the extracted archive directory, run:

    cd 20261110_native311b_onward
    pdflatex -no-shell-escape -halt-on-error -jobname=gauss_ix main.tex
    pdflatex -no-shell-escape -halt-on-error -jobname=gauss_ix main.tex

The result is gauss_ix.pdf in that directory. The archive contains the
107 editable LaTeX inputs, including the modern edition notice and the
delimiter helper. The historical diagrams are drawn in LaTeX/TikZ; no
source scan is needed to compile the text.

The Hannover map uses the Latin Modern TS1 long-s glyph and pdfTeX page
size primitives. It occupies a separate A2 landscape page; subsequent
text pages return to A4. All map elements are editable LaTeX/TikZ.

The original historical portrait is the single raster illustration, gauss_historical_portrait.png, included beside main.tex. It is a required build input; text, mathematics, tables and diagrams are not images.
