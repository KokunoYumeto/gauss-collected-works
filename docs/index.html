Authorship and sources

Carl Friedrich Gauss is the author of the historical mathematical text. Historical editors, correspondents and contributors retain their named credits. Original-language text is AI-transcribed, with AI-produced source comparisons and typesetting; the English and German texts are AI-translated. Exact model credits and source versions remain in the accompanying editions and guides. No human or independent review is claimed.

The translation guide credits H. Maser; James Caddall Morehead and Adam Miller Hiltebeitel; Paul Taylor and Bernard Leak; and the other named terminology references for their stated roles. See the full scholarly credits and citation metadata. AI work does not replace historical or inherited human authorship.

The text follows the Göttingen State and University Library copy (GDZ PPN236010751). The text at the right edge of historical page 95 follows the alternative photograph from Kyushu University Library (item 410995). The relationship between the photographed copies remains undetermined.

The title vignettes in Volumes I, III, IV, VIII and IX use the matching unsigned image from the 1870 second printing of Werke I, digitized from the University of California Libraries copy and marked Public Domain Mark 1.0. The historical Göttingen editions remain the textual witnesses.

Volume VIII was AI-translated into English and modern German and typeset by OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol (Ultra). The original-language transcription and editable mathematical drawings were produced by OpenAI GPT-6 Astra (Ultra).

These editions are freely available for study and teaching.
