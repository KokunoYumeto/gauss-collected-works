\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}. . . . .\quad Finally, as for my method of calculating special perturbations, I gladly consent to your making it public\HistoricalNoteEight{Cf. \textsc{Encke}'s papers in the Berliner Astronomischen Jahrbüchern for 1837 and 1838, especially the note in the former, p. 251.]}, since I do not yet know when or whether I myself might get to it, and this expedient will in any case of itself do away with the indiscreet misuse of private communication to others that might otherwise be feared. I shall find for you my figures for the earlier oppositions of Pallas, together with the magnitudes of the corresponding perturbations of the elements. The papers relating to them have, however, not passed through my hands for a long time.\quad\mbox{. . . . .}
