\[
\begin{gathered}
1)\quad\text{from }2g+\mathfrak A^{(0)}\quad .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\\[.5ex]

\end{gathered}

\[
\begin{gathered}
2)\quad\text{from }\mathfrak A^{(i)}\cos iD\quad .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\\[.5ex]
aa\mathfrak A^{(i)}\left\{\begin{aligned}

&+\tfrac14e\left(\frac1{h+2}-\frac{4h}{hh-1}+\frac3h\right)\cos(iD+M)\\

\[
\begin{gathered}
3)\quad\text{from }e\mathfrak B^{(0)}\cos M\quad .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\, .\\[.5ex]

\begin{gathered}

The parts still to be added [from the last two terms of 15] are:
\hypertarget{native-396}{}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&a(3g+2\mathfrak A^{(0)})nt+\Sigma\frac{a(2\mathfrak A^{(i)}-\tfrac12\alpha^{(i)})}h\sin iD\\
&+(2\mathfrak B^{(0)}-\tfrac12\beta^{(0)})ea\sin M+\Sigma\frac{2\mathfrak B^{(i)}-\tfrac12\beta^{(i)}}{h+1}ea\sin(iD+M)\\

\end{aligned}
\]
\noindent[The coefficient \(\alpha^{(0)}\) has been left aside here because the constant part of \(\int\partial R\) has been denoted by \(g\). Here too, all terms are still to be multiplied by \(\mu\).]

[We shall now carry out a few suitable transformations:

In \(\delta v\) we found the term \(a(3g+2\mathfrak A^{(0)})nt\), which can be made to vanish by a suitable choice of \(g\), so that \(n\) does indeed represent the mean motion.] It follows that
\[g=-\tfrac13a\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}A^{(0)}}{\mathrm{d}a}\right)\mathpunct{\cdot}\]

[Next, the secular terms in \(\delta r\) and \(\delta v\) are to be converted into secular perturbations of the eccentricity and the aphelion. By the formulas above we have
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{the secular part of }\delta r\text{ is}\\[.5ex]
\tfrac14aa(B^{(0)}-2\mathfrak B^{(0)})ent\sin M-\tfrac12aa\mathfrak C^{(0)}e'nt\sin(D+M')\\[1ex]
\text{»}\qquad\text{»}\qquad\text{»}\qquad\text{»}\quad\delta v\quad\text{»}\\[.5ex]
\tfrac12a(\mathfrak B^{(0)}-2\mathfrak B^{(0)})ent\cos M-a\mathfrak C^{(0)}e'nt\cos(D+M')
\end{gathered}
\]
\noindent and since \(D=M-M'+\tilde\omega-\tilde\omega'\), we have
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{the former equal to}\\[.5ex]
\begin{aligned}
&\tfrac14aa\left(B^{(0)}-2\mathfrak B^{(0)}-2\frac{e'}e\mathfrak C^{(0)}\cos(\tilde\omega-\tilde\omega')\right)ent\sin M\\
&\hfill-\tfrac12aa\mathfrak C^{(0)}\sin(\tilde\omega-\tilde\omega')e'nt\cos M
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}
\]
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{the latter equal to}\\[.5ex]
\begin{aligned}
&\tfrac12a\left(B^{(0)}-2\mathfrak B^{(0)}-2\frac{e'}e\mathfrak C^{(0)}\cos(\tilde\omega-\tilde\omega')\right)ent\cos M\\
&\hfill+a\mathfrak C^{(0)}\sin(\tilde\omega-\tilde\omega')e'nt\sin M.
\end{aligned}
\end{gathered}
\]

But from the relations
\[r=a(1+e\cos M)\qquad v=nt+\epsilon-2e\sin M\]
\noindent we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta r&=a\cos M\mathord{.}\,\delta e+ae\sin M\mathord{.}\,\delta\tilde\omega\\
\delta v&=-2\sin M\mathord{.}\,\delta e+2e\cos M\mathord{.}\,\delta\tilde\omega;
\end{aligned}
\]
\noindent\hypertarget{native-397}{}and therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta e&=\tfrac12e'a\mathfrak C^{(0)}nt\sin(\tilde\omega'-\tilde\omega)\\
\delta\tilde\omega&=\tfrac14a\left(B^{(0)}-2\mathfrak B^{(0)}-2\frac{e'}e\mathfrak C^{(0)}\cos(\tilde\omega'-\tilde\omega)\right)nt.]
\end{aligned}
\]
