\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Olbers}, Göttingen, 16 February 1816.
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}. . . . .\quad I am much obliged for the information you sent from the Conn. des tems 1818. I shall leave \textsc{Laplace}'s calculation of the reliability of the value for Jupiter's mass aside, since it depends on his theory of perturbations, in which the accuracy of the results is uncertain. In future the new planets will give us the most accurate knowledge of Jupiter's mass. After centuries have passed, \textsc{Halley}'s comet and yours will also contribute.\quad . . . . .
