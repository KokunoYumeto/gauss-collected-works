\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Gerling}, Göttingen, 8 February 1834.
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}. . . . .\quad I have begun in the last few days (after a break of several years) to observe Pallas as it approaches opposition. \textsc{Encke}'s ephemeris is off by about 5 min. It is painful to think that my work on the perturbations of Pallas, which I did more than 20 years ago, has thus far had to remain without continuation, development, or publication, and will probably perish with me one day, like so much else. You would scarcely believe how hard it is for me to carry a scientific work through when my time is so divided and I am under the pressure of so many circumstances.\quad . . . . .
