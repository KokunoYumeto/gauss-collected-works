\par\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}On [VI.]: In April 1812 \textsc{Gauss} began the second calculation of the
general perturbations. On a slip of paper,
\textsc{Gauss} kept the following journal-like notes on the extent of the individual calculations and the time they took.
Because of their particular interest, they are printed here as faithfully as possible
from the original, with only a few numbers omitted. The signs
\BJournalDay{☾}, \BJournalDay{♂}, \BJournalDay{☿}, \BJournalDay{♃},
\BJournalDay{♀}, \BJournalDay{♄}, \BJournalDay{☉} de\SourceLeaf{611}note, as usual, the
days of the week Monday, Tuesday etc. They made it possible to establish the year beyond doubt.
The entries »complete Aug. 15« etc. give the times
by which, according to the advance estimate on each occasion,
the expansion of the disturbing function was expected to be
complete.
