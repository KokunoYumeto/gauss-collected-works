It is worth illustrating the preceding rules with an example. Let $\AscendingNode=172^0\,28^{\prime}\,13^{\prime\prime}{,}7$ and $i=34^0\,38^{\prime}\,1^{\prime\prime}{,}1$. Further, let the new plane be parallel to the equator, so that $n=180^0$. Let the angle $\epsilon$, the obliquity of the ecliptic, be $=23^0\,27^{\prime}\,55^{\prime\prime}{,}8$. We thus have

$i-\epsilon=$ & $11\quad10\quad5{,}3$\\[.3em]

\noindent We therefore obtain $\frac{1}{2}i'=5^0\,51^{\prime}\,56^{\prime\prime}{,}445$, $i'=11^0\,43^{\prime}\,52^{\prime\prime}{,}89$,\hfil\break $\AscendingNode'=338^0\,30^{\prime}\,50^{\prime\prime}{,}44$, $\Delta=-14^0\,52^{\prime}\,12^{\prime\prime}{,}42$. Moreover, the point $n$ on the celestial sphere clearly corresponds- to the autumnal equinox. Thus the distance of the ascending node of the orbit on the equator from the vernal equinox (its \textit{right ascension}) is $158^0\,30^{\prime}\,50^{\prime\prime}{,}44$.

\SourceLeaf{075}To illustrate Article 53 we shall carry this example further and develop formulas for the coordinates relative to three planes passing through the Sun. Let one plane be parallel to the equator and let the positive poles of the other two have right ascensions $0^0$ and $90^0$. Let the distances from these planes be $z$, $x$, and $y$, respectively. If the distances on the celestial sphere from the heliocentric position to the points $\AscendingNode$ and $\AscendingNode'$ are further denoted by $u$ and $u'$, respectively, then $u'=u-\Delta=u+14^0\,52^{\prime}\,12^{\prime\prime}{,}42$, and the quantities represented in Article 53 by $i$, $N-\AscendingNode$, and $u$ are here $i'$, $180^0-\AscendingNode'$, and $u'$. The formulas given there thus yield
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\makebox[18em][l]{$\log a\sin A$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}968\,7197_n$\\[.3em]
\makebox[18em][l]{$\log a\cos A$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}554\,6380_n$\\[.3em]
\hline
whence $A=$ & $248^0\,55^{\prime}\,22^{\prime\prime}{,}97$\\[.3em]
\makebox[18em][l]{$\log a$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}998\,7923$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\makebox[18em][l]{$\log b\sin B$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}563\,8058$\\[.3em]
\makebox[18em][l]{$\log b\cos B$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}959\,5519_n$\\[.3em]
\hline
whence $B=$ & $158^0\,5^{\prime}\,54^{\prime\prime}{,}97$\\[.3em]
\makebox[18em][l]{$\log b$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}992\,0848$

\noindent We thus have
\[
\begin{aligned}
x&=ar\sin(u'+248^0\,55^{\prime}\,22^{\prime\prime}{,}97)\\
&=ar\sin(u+263^0\,47^{\prime}\,35^{\prime\prime}{,}39)\\[.4em]
y&=br\sin(u'+158\quad5\quad54{,}97)\\
&=br\sin(u+172\quad58\quad7{,}39)\\[.4em]
z&=cr\sin u'\\
&=cr\sin(u+14\quad52\quad12{,}42),
\end{aligned}
\]
where $\log c=\log\sin i'=9{,}308\,1870$.
