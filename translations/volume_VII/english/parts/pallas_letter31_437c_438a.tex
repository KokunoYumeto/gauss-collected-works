\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}. . . . .\quad As for the tables for Pallas and the latest elements, all the papers relating to them are so scattered that it is quite impossible for me just now to study them again closely enough to give directions for a \SourceLeaf{438}reliable computation. Unless something else intervenes to suspend or prevent the continuation of my measurements this year, the astronomers will have to manage as best they can on this occasion.\quad . . . . .
