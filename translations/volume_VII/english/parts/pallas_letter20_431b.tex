\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}. . . . .\quad My work on the perturbations of Pallas has been entirely at a standstill for some time, but I shall soon return to it and work out the theory. The coming opposition looks likely to fit in very well. I am curious whether Saturn will once again spoil that fine agreement a little even after this opposition has been included.\quad . . . . .
