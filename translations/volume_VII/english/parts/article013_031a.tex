\emph{Example.} Let the eccentricity be the same as in the example in Article 10, and let
\[
M = 332^0\,28^{\prime}\,54^{\prime\prime}{,}76.
\]
For the eccentricity expressed in arcseconds, $\log e$ is then $4{,}704\,1513$, so that $e=50600^{\prime\prime}=14^0\,3^{\prime}\,20^{\prime\prime}$. Since $E$ must be smaller than $M$ in this case, we set $\epsilon=326^0$ for the first calculation. From the smaller tables we obtain
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{13em}@{}}
\makebox[7em][l]{$\log\sin\epsilon$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.\,.} & $9{,}74756_n,$ & change per $1^{\prime}$ .\,.\,.\,.\,. $19$, whence $\lambda=0{,}32$\\[.3em]
\makebox[7em][l]{$\log e$ in arcsec\hfill .\,.\,.} & $4{,}70415$ & \\
\cline{1-2}
 & $4{,}45171_n$ &
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{12em}@{}}
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{hence $e\sin\epsilon=-28295^{\prime\prime}=-7^0\,51^{\prime}\,35^{\prime\prime}$.} & Change in the logarithm per tabular unit, here equivalent to $10$\\
$M+e\sin\epsilon=$ & $324\ \ 37\ \ 20$ & arcseconds, .\,.\,.\,.\,. $16$, whence $\mu=1{,}6$\\[.3em]
Difference from $\epsilon$ .\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,. & $1\ \ 22\ \ 40=4960^{\prime\prime}$. &
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent Hence $\frac{0{,}32}{1{,}28}\times4960^{\prime\prime}=1240^{\prime\prime}=20^{\prime}\,40^{\prime\prime}$. Thus we obtain the corrected value $E$~$=324^0\,37^{\prime}\,20^{\prime\prime}-20^{\prime}\,40^{\prime\prime}=324^0\,16^{\prime}\,40^{\prime\prime}$. With this value we repeat the calculation using the larger tables.

\makebox[9em][l]{$\log e$\hfill .\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.\,.} & $4{,}704\,1513$ & \\
\cline{1-2}
 & $4{,}470\,4571_n$ & $\mu=147$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\multicolumn{2}{l}{$e\sin\epsilon=-29543^{\prime\prime}{,}17=-8^0\,12^{\prime}\,23^{\prime\prime}{,}17$}\\[.3em]

\noindent Multiplying this difference by $\frac{\lambda}{\mu-\lambda}=\frac{29{,}25}{117{,}75}$ gives $2^{\prime\prime}{,}09$ and hence the newly corrected value $E=324^0\,16^{\prime}\,31^{\prime\prime}{,}59-2^{\prime\prime}{,}09=324^0\,16^{\prime}\,29^{\prime\prime}{,}50$, accurate to within $0^{\prime\prime}{,}01$.
