\begin{center}\large REMARKS.\end{center}
In the foregoing reprint of the Theoria motus, particular care has been taken to correct
all typographical errors and other inaccuracies. In addition to those already generally
known, many newly discovered ones have been taken into account. The numerical examples
were recalculated, which also revealed a considerable number of places that needed
correction. Very often the numerical values obtained using modern logarithm tables
differed by one or two units in the last place. In most cases this is probably because
the older tables used to calculate these examples gave no decimal places in their
tables of proportional parts (cf. the discussion in Art. 31). In some cases these small
discrepancies lead to larger differences as the calculation proceeds. For example, on
page 35 the quantity $N$ was found to be 0,012\,9939, only one unit in the seventh
decimal place less than the value given by \textsc{Gauss}. Since \textsc{Gauss} also
gives the corresponding logarithm to seven places, however, the difference here is
33 units in the last place (namely $\log N = 8{,}113\,7396$), and the final result is
$t = 13{,}91438$ instead of $13{,}91448$. Similarly, the last digits in the example on
the right-hand side of page 36 and elsewhere are uncertain. Since these differences
fall within the limits of precision of the calculation, no changes were made in such
cases. The same applies to all other insignificant differences found in the last
decimal places.

The places where a correction was made are listed below. Corrections already noted in
the German edition (»Theorie der Bewegung der Himmelskörper etc.«, by C.\,F.
\textsc{Gauss}, translated into German by C. \textsc{Haase}, Hannover 1865), as well as
changes in the spelling and grammar of the text alone, are generally omitted to keep
the list from becoming excessively long. The typographical errors in the tables,
however, have all been listed. An appended G. means that \textsc{Gauss} himself pointed
out the error in question.
