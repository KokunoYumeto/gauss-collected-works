\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Olbers}, Göttingen, 31 March 1818.
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}. . . . .\quad Since my tables [of the perturbations] that Pallas undergoes from Jupiter are now quite complete and the elements have been fitted as closely as possible to all 12 oppositions to date, I have asked Mr. \textsc{Encke} to calculate all the observations from 1802 using these elements. Although you discovered the planet only some time after the opposition, it may still be possible to derive the opposition from the observations now that the elements have been determined so precisely.\quad . . . . .
