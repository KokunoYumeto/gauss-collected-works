I take this opportunity also to announce a correction to be made on pp. 218 and] 219 [pp. 250--252 of this volume] of the Theoria Motus Corporum Coelestium. Read as follows
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{p. 218 l. 3 instead of}\quad e^{-\frac{hh\sigma\sigma}{\delta'''}}\quad\text{. . . .}\quad e^{-hh\delta'''\sigma\sigma}\\[5pt]
&\phantom{\text{p. 218 }}\text{l. 4 instead of}\quad\BareRoot{\frac{1}{\delta'''}}\quad\text{. . . . . .}\quad\BareRoot{\delta'''}\\[5pt]
&\text{p. 219 l. 8 instead of}\quad\BareRoot{A},\BareRoot{B'},\BareRoot{C''},\BareRoot{D'''}\\[4pt]
&\qquad\text{in the same order}\quad
\frac{1}{\BareRoot{A}}\mathpunct{\raisebox{.45ex}{,}}\quad
\frac{1}{\BareRoot{B'}}\mathpunct{\raisebox{.45ex}{,}}\quad
\frac{1}{\BareRoot{C''}}\mathpunct{\raisebox{.45ex}{,}}\quad
\frac{1}{\BareRoot{D'''}}\mathpunct{\cdot}
\end{aligned}
\]

This inaccuracy arose because different notation had been used earlier in the investigation, and its replacement had been overlooked at the indicated places. The numerical example, as can be seen, is calculated in accordance with the corrected expressions, but an error of calculation was made in it. Specifically, the equation on p. 219, line 5 from the bottom, must be
\[
6633r=12707+2P-9Q+123R,
\]
consequently the precision of \(r\) on p. 220
\[
=\BareRoot{\frac{2211}{41}}=7{,}34.
\]

Mr. \textsc{Nicolai} drew my attention to these corrections, for which I here express my most grateful thanks to him.
