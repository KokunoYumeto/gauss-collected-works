\begin{center}
Determination of the probable errors of the results\\
of the method of least squares.
\end{center}
Suppose that the algorithm taught in vol. I of the G[öttinger] C[ommenta\-tiones: Disquisitio de elementis ellipticis Palladis, Werke, Volume VI, pp. 21--22] leads to the following equations for determining the unknown \SourceLeaf{314}quantities \(p,q,r,s\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
\relax[A=]\;0&=\lambda+\alpha p+\beta q+\gamma r+\delta s\\
[B=]\;0&=\lambda'\phantom{+\alpha p}+\beta'q+\gamma'r+\delta's\\
[C=]\;0&=\lambda''\phantom{+\alpha p+\beta q}+\gamma''r+\delta''s\\
[D=]\;0&=\lambda'''\phantom{+\alpha p+\beta q+\gamma r}+\delta'''s.
\end{aligned}
\]

Determine \(\pi,\chi,\rho,\sigma\) by the equations
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha\pi=\text{Prob. obs. error}\\
&\beta\pi+\beta'\chi=0\\
&\gamma\pi+\gamma'\chi+\gamma''\rho=0\\
&\delta\pi+\delta'\chi+\delta''\rho+\delta'''\sigma=0,
\end{aligned}
\]
then the probable error of \(p\) is:
\[
\BareRoot{(\alpha\pi\pi+\beta'\chi\chi+\gamma''\rho\rho+\delta'''\sigma\sigma)}.
\]
Still more elegantly as follows: let elimination from the equations
\[
\begin{aligned}
\alpha p+\beta q+\gamma r+\delta s&=u\\
\beta'q+\gamma'r+\delta's&=u'\\
\gamma''r+\delta''s&=u''\\
\delta'''s&=u'''
\end{aligned}
\]
give
\[
\begin{aligned}
p&=p^0u+p'u'+p''u''+p'''u'''\\
q&=\phantom{p^0u+{}}q'u'+q''u''+q'''u'''\\
r&=\phantom{p^0u+p'u'+{}}r''u''+r'''u'''\\
s&=\phantom{p^0u+p'u'+p''u''+{}}s'''u''';
\end{aligned}
\]
then the most probable values are
\[
\begin{aligned}
-p&=p^0\lambda+p'\lambda'+p''\lambda''+p'''\lambda'''\\
-q&=\phantom{p^0\lambda+{}}q'\lambda'+q''\lambda''+q'''\lambda'''\\
-r&=\phantom{p^0\lambda+p'\lambda'+{}}r''\lambda''+r'''\lambda'''\\
-s&=\phantom{p^0\lambda+p'\lambda'+p''\lambda''+{}}s'''\lambda'''
\end{aligned}
\]
and the probable errors of these determinations, setting the probable error of the observations \(=1\):
\SourceLeaf{315}
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{for }p\;\text{. . . . .}\quad&\BareRoot{(\alpha p^0p^0+\beta'p'p'+\gamma''p''p''+\delta'''p'''p''')}\\[5pt]
\text{» }q\;\text{. . . . .}\quad&\BareRoot{(\beta'q'q'+\gamma''q''q''+\delta'''q'''q''')}\\[5pt]
\text{» }r\;\text{. . . . .}\quad&\BareRoot{(\gamma''r''r''+\delta'''r'''r''')}\\[5pt]
\text{» }s\;\text{. . . . .}\quad&s'''\BareRoot{\delta'''}=\BareRoot{\frac{1}{\delta'''}}\\[10pt]
\text{» }r\;\text{. . . . .}\quad&\BareRoot{\left(\frac{1}{\gamma''}+\frac{\delta''\delta''}{\gamma''\gamma''\delta'''}\right)}\mathpunct{\cdot}
\end{aligned}
\]
