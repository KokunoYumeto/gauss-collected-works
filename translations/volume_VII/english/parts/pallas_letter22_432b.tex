\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}\{. . . . .\quad Has the discrepancy been cleared up, given that the perturbations by Saturn seemed to disrupt the excellent agreement among the oppositions of Pallas, which according to your last communication had been restored when Jupiter's mass was slightly increased? — I do hope . . . . . you will communicate your method to the Paris Institute. The prize was kept open so long solely on your account. I hear nothing from Paris at present.\quad . . . . .\}
