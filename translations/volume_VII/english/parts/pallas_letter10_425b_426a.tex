\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}\{I have only now seen the Moniteur of 9 June 1811, and I hasten to write to you with more details about the prizes, if you have not already heard. Two papers on the perturbations of Pallas have been submitted. One is given no consideration at all, while the other shows considerable expertise in analysis, yet has failed to meet the requirements. The deadline has therefore been extended by a further 5 years, until 1 \SourceLeaf{426}June 1816, and the Institute will award the prize to the first paper received within that time that meets the conditions in full. The prize remains the same, namely 6000 francs.\}
