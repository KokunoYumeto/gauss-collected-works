\begin{center}
\pdfbookmark[2]{Gauss to Schumacher, Göttingen, 6 January 1811}{pallas-brief-18110106}
\textsc{Gauss} to \textsc{Schumacher}, Göttingen, 6 January 1811.
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}.\quad.\quad.\quad.\quad.\quad If the Paris Institute sets the perturbations of Pallas as a prize problem for the fourth time, I am not entirely disinclined to consider taking it up, since the subject has now become of interest to me in its own right. I doubt, however, that this will happen. Since you read the Moniteur yourself there, I ask you to be so kind as to let me know if you come across anything on the subject in its pages over the next few days.
\par\bigskip
\begin{center}
\pdfbookmark[2]{Olbers to Gauss, Bremen, 26 January 1811}{pallas-brief-18110126}
\textsc{Olbers} to \textsc{Gauss}, Bremen, 26 January 1811.
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}\{.\quad.\quad.\quad.\quad.\quad It gave me the greatest pleasure to see from the Moniteur that the deadline for the 6000-franc prize for the perturbations of Pallas has been extended until 1 October 1816, with the provision that the prize shall be awarded to the first paper received during this period that meets the intentions and requirements of those who set the prize problem. Now I have no doubt\quad.\quad.\quad. that, after your remark, which gave me such pleasure, you will undertake the complete solution of this problem.\quad.\quad.\quad.\quad.\quad.\}
