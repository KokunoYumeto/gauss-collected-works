\textsc{Encke} to \textsc{Gauss}, Berlin, 4 October 1834.
\end{center}
\noindent\hspace*{\parindent}\{. . . . .\quad When you were kind enough to present to me your method of partial perturbations in 1811, you communicated it with the remark that you did not wish it to be circulated, as you intended to publish something about it yourself. I have used it continually since then, and the yearbook now requires me to keep applying it to the four minor planets and also to the short-period comet. \textsc{Heiligenstein}'s death has made it necessary for Ceres as well, which has been done during this recess. I foresee, however, that I shall no longer be able to manage on my own, and that even if one or another of your pupils were willing to take on one planet or another, this would not help me, since I shall most probably be obliged to entrust even the work on the comet to someone else. I should therefore like to ask your permission to communicate your methods to anyone to whom I entrust such work, since I can get no help without being free to communicate them. I would be still more delighted if you would allow me to present the method in detail, perhaps in an appendix to the yearbook.\quad\mbox{. . . . .\}}
