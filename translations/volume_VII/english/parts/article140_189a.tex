First, observe that if $N$ is any point on the great circle $CC'C''$ and the distances of the points $C,C',C''$ from $N$ are reckoned in the same direction, from $C$ towards $C''$, so that in general
\[
NC''-NC'=2f,\qquad NC''-NC=2f',\qquad NC'-NC=2f'',
\]
then we have the equation
\[
\text{I.}\qquad 0=\sin2f\sin NC-\sin2f'\sin NC'+\sin2f''\sin NC''.
\]
Now suppose that $N$ is taken at the intersection of the great circles $BB^*B''$, $CC'C''$, as the ascending node of the former on the latter. Let $\mathfrak{C},\mathfrak{C}',\mathfrak{C}'',\mathfrak{D},\mathfrak{D}',\mathfrak{D}''$ denote, in the order given, the distances of the points $C,C',C'',D,D',D''$ from the great circle $BB^*B''$, taken as positive on one side and negative on the opposite side. Clearly $\sin\mathfrak{C},\sin\mathfrak{C}',\sin\mathfrak{C}''$ are then proportional respectively to $\sin NC,\sin NC',\sin NC''$, so that equation I takes the form
\[
0=\sin2f\sin\mathfrak{C}-\sin2f'\sin\mathfrak{C}'+\sin2f''\sin\mathfrak{C}''
\]
or, on multiplying by $rr'r''$,
\hypertarget{native-188}{}
\[
\text{II.}\qquad 0=nr\sin\mathfrak{C}-n'r'\sin\mathfrak{C}'+n''r''\sin\mathfrak{C}''.
\]
Furthermore, $\sin\mathfrak{C}$ is plainly to $\sin\mathfrak{D}'$ as the sine of the distance of $C$ from $B$ is to the sine of the distance of $D'$ from $B$, with both distances measured in the same direction. Thus we have
\[
-\sin\mathfrak{C}=\frac{\sin\mathfrak{D}'\sin CB}{\sin(AD'-\delta)}\mathpunct{\raisebox{.45ex}{,}}
\]
and in exactly the same way we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
-\sin\mathfrak{C}&=\frac{\sin\mathfrak{D}''\sin CB}{\sin(AD''-\delta)}\\[.8ex]
-\sin\mathfrak{C}'&=\frac{\sin\mathfrak{D}\sin C'B^*}{\sin(A'D-\delta'+\sigma)}
=\frac{\sin\mathfrak{D}''\sin C'B^*}{\sin(A'D''-\delta'+\sigma)}\\[.8ex]
-\sin\mathfrak{C}''&=\frac{\sin\mathfrak{D}\sin C''B''}{\sin(A''D-\delta'')}
=\frac{\sin\mathfrak{D}'\sin C''B''}{\sin(A''D'-\delta'')}\mathpunct{\cdot}
\end{aligned}
\]
Dividing equation II by $r''\sin\mathfrak{C}''$ gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
0={}&n\cdot\frac{r\sin CB}{r''\sin C''B''}\cdot\frac{\sin A''D'-\delta''}{\sin(AD'-\delta)}\\[.8ex]
&-n'\cdot\frac{r'\sin C'B^*}{r''\sin C''B''}\cdot\frac{\sin(A''D-\delta'')}{\sin(A'D-\delta'+\sigma)}+n''.
\end{aligned}
\]
If we now denote the arc $C'B'$ by $z$, substitute for $r,r',r''$ their values from the preceding article, and for brevity set
\[
\begin{aligned}
11)\quad&\frac{R\sin\delta\sin(A''D'-\delta'')}{R''\sin\delta''\sin(AD'-\delta)}=a\\[.8ex]
12)\quad&\frac{R'\sin\delta'\sin(A''D-\delta'')}{R''\sin\delta''\sin(A'D-\delta'+\sigma)}=b,
\end{aligned}
\]
our equation takes the form
\[
\text{III.}\qquad 0=an-bn'\cdot\frac{\sin(z-\sigma)}{\sin z}+n''.
\]
The coefficient $b$ can also be computed by the following formula, which is easily derived from the equations just given.
\[
13)\qquad a\cdot\frac{R'\sin\delta'\sin(AD''-\delta)}{R\sin\delta\sin(A'D''-\delta'+\sigma)}=b.
\]
It is worth using both formulas 12 and 13 to check the calculation.\HistoricalUnmarkedNote{[Manuscript note:] For Fig. 4: Let $P$ be the pole of the great circle $CC'C''$. Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(\frac{n''\cos BP}{N''\sin\beta}-1\right)\sin A'D''.\sin B'D\\[.7ex]
&\qquad=\left(\frac{n\cos B''P}{N\sin\beta''}-1\right)\sin A'D.\sin B'D''
\end{aligned}
\]
[where $N,N''$ are twice the areas of the triangles between the radius vectors of the Earth.]}
\hypertarget{native-189}{}Whenever $\sin(A'D''-\delta'+\sigma)$ is greater than $\sin(A'D-\delta'+\sigma)$, the latter formula will be less affected by the unavoidable errors of the tables than the former and should therefore be preferred if a small discrepancy attributable to those errors appears in the values of $b$. Conversely, the former formula is more reliable whenever $\sin(A'D''-\delta'+\sigma)$ is less than $\sin(A'D-\delta'+\sigma)$. If preferred, a suitable mean of the two values can be adopted.

The following formulas can also be used to check the calculation. We omit their derivation for brevity, although it is fairly straightforward.
\[
\begin{aligned}
0&=\frac{a\sin(l''-l')}{R}-\frac{b\sin(l''-l)}{R'}\cdot\frac{\sin(\delta'-\sigma)}{\sin\delta'}+\frac{\sin(l'-l)}{R''}\\[.8ex]
b&=\frac{R'\sin\delta'}{R''\sin\delta''}\cdot\frac{U\cos\beta\cos\beta''}{\sin(AD'-\delta)\sin\epsilon'}\mathpunct{\raisebox{.45ex}{,}}
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $U$ denotes the quotient $\frac{S}{\sin(\delta'-\sigma)}=\frac{T\sin(t+\gamma')}{\cos(\delta'-\sigma)}$ (Article 138, equation 10).
